Women's Safety

Your safety is our priority. Know your rights and resources.

Emergency Helplines

  • Women's Helpline: 1091
  • Police Emergency: 100
  • Ambulance: 102
  • College Security: 1800-123-4567
  • Counseling Support: 1800-987-6543

Safety Tips

  • ✅ Always share your location with friends/family
  • ✅ Save emergency contacts on speed dial
  • ✅ Avoid isolated areas after dark
  • ✅ Use college transport services when available
  • ✅ Report any suspicious activity immediately

Legal Rights & Policies

Your college has a zero-tolerance policy against harassment. You have the right to:

  • Report harassment without fear of retaliation
  • Confidential counseling and support services
  • Fair investigation of complaints
  • Access to legal aid and representation
