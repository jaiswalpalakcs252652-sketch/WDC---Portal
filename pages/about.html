About the Committee

Empowering Women Through Education and Support

Our Vision

To create a safe, inclusive, and empowering campus environment where every woman student can thrive academically, personally, and professionally.

Our Mission

  • Promote gender equality and women's empowerment
  • Provide safety resources and support systems
  • Organize workshops and events for skill development
  • Address grievances confidentially and effectively
